**Notes before you publish**

- **Remove any "guaranteed," "risk-free," or "double your money" language.** These claims are a common basis for FTC and SEC action against trading services.
- **Any performance figures must be verified and disclosed.** Show net-of-fees results over a stated period, and don't cherry-pick. The SEC's Marketing Rule governs this if you're a registered adviser, and the FTC applies its own standards.
- **Check whether you need to register.** Paid, ongoing stock recommendations can make you an investment adviser depending on your jurisdiction and how the service is structured. Have a securities attorney review the page before it goes live.
- **Add a full disclaimer** in the footer covering risk of loss, the non-personalized nature of the alerts, and that members make their own decisions.
