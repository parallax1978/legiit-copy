## 7 Email Subject Line Templates

These are for the follow-up email that delivers or promotes the offer. Each one fits a different angle, so you can test them against each other.

1. **Curiosity:** `{first_name}, why do some emails get opened and others don't?`
2. **Direct benefit:** `Get more opens from your next {number} emails`
3. **Continuation:** `Now that you have [Lead Magnet Name], here's the next step`
4. **Diagnostic:** `Your open rate is probably [X]%. Here's how to find out why.`
5. **Question:** `Are your subject lines doing the work, or is your sender name?`
6. **Reminder:** `The email open rate course is still open to you, {first_name}`
7. **Final notice:** `Last note about the open rate mini-course`

**Notes on use**
- Send the offer email within an hour of the purchase, while the lead magnet is still fresh.
- Keep the wording honest. If you don't have a real deadline, don't write one. Templates 6 and 7 work without a fake countdown.
- Swap in real numbers, such as your own open rate or the count of lessons, only after you've confirmed them.
