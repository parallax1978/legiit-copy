## Before you publish, check Meta's ad policies

- **Don't say the viewer has ADHD.** Meta rejects copy like "Do you have ADHD?" or "Struggling with your ADHD?" because it implies you know a personal health attribute. The copy above describes who the *product* is for ("built for ADHD brains"), not who the *viewer* is.
- **Make no medical claims.** Avoid "treats," "cures," or "manages symptoms." Present it as a productivity tool, not a therapy.
- **Fix the bullets to match the real app.** I made up the feature list, so swap in what the app actually does. Also confirm the 7-day free trial exists, or remove that line.
