### Creative ideas
- **Image:** A phone screen showing a Google search for "[practice area] lawyer near me," with the firm's spot circled at #1. Overlay text: *"Is this you?"*

### Tips for running it
- **Targeting:** Use interests like law firm owner, managing partner, and attorney, or upload a custom list of law firm contacts. Choose Lead Form or a booking-page conversion objective.
- **Compliance:** Meta's policies and legal marketing rules both penalize promises like "guaranteed #1 rankings," so the copy avoids them. Only add specific results, like "212% more leads for a PI firm," if you have real case studies to back them up.
- **Test:** Run A and C against each other first. The offer framing usually decides the winner more than the wording does.
