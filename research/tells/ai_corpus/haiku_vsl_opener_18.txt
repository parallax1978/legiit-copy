### Production and Compliance Notes

- **Replace all claims with verifiable data.** Don't say "save thousands" unless you can show a real example from your own closed loans, and disclose the assumptions behind it.
- **Check your state and federal advertising rules.** Mortgage ads typically require your NMLS number and company disclosures. Some states also have rules about rate and payment claims, so have your compliance team review the script before it goes live.
- **Avoid "guaranteed" language.** Rates, approval, and savings depend on the borrower's situation, and the script should not imply otherwise.
- **Get consent for any real homeowner footage**, and don't present actors as real clients.
- **Test the 2:00 runtime** by reading the script aloud at a natural pace. Trim the problem section first if it runs long.
