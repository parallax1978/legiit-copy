# VSL Script: SEO for Law Firms, First 2 Minutes

**Runtime:** about 2:00, roughly 310 spoken words at a normal pace
**Goal:** Hook the viewer, make the problem hurt, build credibility, and set up the free strategy call

---

**[0:00–0:12] HOOK**

*[VISUAL: Black screen. White text appears word by word as it's spoken.]*

**VO:**
Right now, someone in your city is typing "car accident lawyer near me" into Google.

They're hurt. They're scared. And they're about to call the first firm they see.

The question is: is that firm yours?

---

**[0:12–0:40] THE PROBLEM**

*[VISUAL: Screen recording of a Google search. The map pack loads and three competitor firms appear. Cursor hovers over the top result.]*

**VO:**
Here's what most attorneys don't realize. Over [X]% of people looking for a lawyer start with a search engine. And most of them never scroll past the first few results.

So if your firm isn't in that top three, you're not losing *some* cases.

You're invisible.

And the firm that *is* showing up? They might not be better lawyers than you. They might have half your experience. They just figured out how Google decides who gets the call.

---

**[0:40–1:05] AGITATE**

*[VISUAL: Montage: a phone ringing at a competitor's front desk, an empty intake calendar, a credit card bill for "Legal Lead Gen Service."]*

**VO:**
Maybe you've already tried to fix this.

You paid an agency that sent you reports full of "impressions" and "keyword movement," but the phone didn't ring any more often.

Maybe you're buying shared leads that get sold to five other firms before you can even call back.

Or maybe you're pouring money into ads, and the second you stop paying, the cases stop coming.

That's not a marketing strategy. That's renting your clients from Google.

---

**[1:05–1:35] CREDIBILITY + TURN**

*[VISUAL: Presenter on camera, in an office or studio. Lower-third: "[Name], Founder, [Agency Name]"]*

**PRESENTER:**
My name is [Name], and for the past [X] years my team has done one thing: help law firms own the top of Google in their market.

Not dentists. Not plumbers. Law firms.

We've worked with [personal injury / family law / criminal defense] firms across [X] states, and we've seen the same pattern over and over. The firms that win online aren't spending the most. They're doing three specific things their competitors aren't.

*[VISUAL: Text overlay: "[Client Firm]: [verified result, e.g. 'signed cases up X% in Y months']"]*

---

**[1:35–2:00] BRIDGE TO OFFER**

*[VISUAL: Presenter, tighter shot.]*

**PRESENTER:**
In the next few minutes, I'll walk you through those three things, so you can see exactly why your firm isn't ranking and what it would take to change that.

And at the end, I'll show you how to get a free, no-obligation strategy call where we look at *your* market, *your* competitors, and *your* website, and hand you a custom plan to start getting more signed cases from search.

Even if you never work with us.

So let's start with the first one, the mistake costing most firms [X] cases a month…

*[TRANSITION to Section 2]*

### Notes before production
- **Fill every bracket with real data.** Use only verified statistics and client results you have permission to share. Firms are a skeptical audience, and invented numbers will hurt you.
- **Don't promise rankings or case volumes.** Google can't be guaranteed, and your audience knows that better than most.
- **Change the search in the hook** to fit the practice area you're targeting, for example "DUI lawyer near me" or "divorce attorney [city]." One niche per VSL converts best.
- **The "three things" open loop** needs a payoff. A good fit for those three would be Google Business Profile and map pack dominance, practice-area content depth, and reviews plus local authority signals.

I can write the next section covering the three things, or the full close and call-to-action, if you'd like.
