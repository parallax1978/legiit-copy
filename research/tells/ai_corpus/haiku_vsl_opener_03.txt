## Production Notes

- **Claims:** I kept the language around "supporting" digestion and "comfortable" gut function rather than saying it treats, cures, or prevents bloating or any condition. Health claims need substantiation, and the FTC and FDA hold supplement advertising to that standard. Any specific benefit, such as "reduces bloating by 40%," needs clinical evidence for that exact strain and dose. If you have a study, share it and I'll work it in. If you don't, the script stays on support language.
- **Placeholders:** Fill in the product name, CFU count, strain names, and the bottle count. The "$1.30 a day" figure assumes a 30-day supply. Check the math against your actual count.
- **Disclaimer:** Add the standard "These statements have not been evaluated by the FDA..." line and a disclaimer on screen. A "consult your doctor" line is also a good idea, especially if the audience includes people with IBS, IBD, or who are pregnant.
- **Testimonials:** Don't add any in this section without real, documented customers. Fabricated reviews are a legal risk.
