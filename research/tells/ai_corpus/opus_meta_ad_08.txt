### Meta compliance tips
- **Don't make income claims** like "Make $10K/month" or "Quit your job." Meta often rejects these, and they can draw FTC scrutiny.
- **Don't use "you" phrasing that implies personal traits**, such as "Are you broke?" or "Struggling with money?" It breaks Meta's personal attributes policy.
- **Don't use Amazon's logo** or suggest you're affiliated with Amazon. Saying "Amazon FBA" descriptively is fine.
