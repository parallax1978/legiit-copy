### Production notes

- **Compliance:** If you add earnings figures or student results, use only real, documented numbers with a typical-results disclaimer. The FTC treats income claims in business-opportunity marketing very strictly. Because of that, I kept this section focused on the process rather than promised income.
- **Placeholders:** Fill in everything in [BRACKETS] before recording. That includes the 1:50 teaser, which should be a real story from your own selling experience.
- **Next section (2:00 onward):** This usually covers the host's origin story, social proof, a pass at the main objection ("Isn't Amazon too saturated?"), and a second CTA.
