## Compliance Notes

These are not optional. Stock-picking subscriptions attract regulatory attention.

1. **Track record:** If you show results, show all the calls, including the losers, over a defined period. Cherry-picked wins can be deceptive. In the U.S., hypothetical or back-tested performance has its own disclosure rules.
2. **Testimonials:** Any testimonial must reflect a typical experience or carry a clear earnings disclaimer. Do not use invented or paid-for results without disclosure.
3. **Registration:** Depending on your jurisdiction and whether you're paid per subscriber, you may need to qualify as a bona fide publisher or be registered as an investment adviser. Check this with a securities attorney before the VSL goes live.
4. **Conflicts:** Disclose any positions you hold in alerted stocks, and whether you're paid by the companies you cover.
5. **Claims:** Avoid words like "guaranteed," "risk-free," "never lose," or specific income promises such as "$3,000 a month."
