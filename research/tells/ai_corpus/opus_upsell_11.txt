## A few notes on the choices

- **The math checks out.** $12 × 12 = $144, and 10 paid months = $120. The page uses $24 and $10/mo everywhere so nobody has to do the math.
- **The bracketed lines need your input.** Most important is the credit for today's $12. Without it, buyers can feel double-charged and you'll see refund requests. The renewal reminder and guarantee are also strong trust signals for this audience. Only keep them if they're true.
- **The decline link is neutral on purpose.** Avoid guilt-trip opt-outs like "No, I like being disorganized." ADHD buyers often carry shame about this, and those links tend to cause refunds and resentment.
- **There's no countdown timer or fake scarcity.** Impulsive buying is a real ADHD pain point, and this audience is quick to call out manipulation. "Only on this page" is enough urgency, as long as it's true.
- **It's short and easy to scan,** with a clear yes or no at the end. The page is written for the way this audience reads.
