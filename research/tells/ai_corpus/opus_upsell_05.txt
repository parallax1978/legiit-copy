### Here's something most Shopify owners don't know

[Product Name] keeps your books accurate **from today forward**. But AI learns from the data you give it. If your past records are messy, it starts from a bad base, and every report it builds sits on top of that.

And for most Shopify stores, the past records *are* messy:

- **Payouts don't match sales.** Shopify sends one lump deposit after fees, refunds, and chargebacks, so your bank account and your revenue never agree.
- **Fees are hidden or miscategorized.** Payment processing, app subscriptions, and Shopify fees are scattered across statements or lumped into "Other."
- **Refunds and chargebacks never got recorded.** That makes your revenue look higher than it really was.
- **Sales tax is a guess.** Collected tax mixed in with revenue is a common and expensive mistake.
- **Personal and business spending got mixed.** It happens to almost everyone in year one.

If any of that sounds familiar, your profit number is probably wrong. You can't make good decisions about ad spend, inventory, or pricing with a profit number you can't trust.
